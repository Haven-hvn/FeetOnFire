Play

Gameplay test page (phase 5). Pick a song from your disk, choose a chart, play. Files stay on this machine; nothing is uploaded. The song list, settings and calibration screens come in phase 6.

This page is not cross-origin isolated, so shared memory is off and the game cannot run. Open it through npm run serve.

1. Song

A folder may hold one song or many. With files, pick the .ssc or .sm file together with its music file.


  


  

    
2. Options

    

      
      
      
      
      
    

    
Speed: 2x (times the song's tempo), C450 (constant 450 BPM) or M450 (the fastest part at 450 BPM). Keys: arrow keys, or D F J K for left, down, up, right. Esc stops.

    
Global offset: as in StepMania, negative makes the notes come later. If you are told you are late when you step on what you hear, make it more negative. A calibration screen comes in phase 6.
